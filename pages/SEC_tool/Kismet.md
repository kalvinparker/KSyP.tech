Kismet is a powerful wireless network detector that can passively scan for 802.11a, b, and g networks in your area. It's a valuable tool for network administrators, security professionals, and anyone interested in understanding the wireless networks around them.

**Key Features:**

* **Passive Scanning:** Kismet detects wireless networks without sending any packets, making it less likely to be detected by intrusion detection systems.
* **Protocol Support:** Supports a wide range of wireless protocols, including 802.11a, b, g, and n.
* **Channel Hopping:** Automatically hops between channels to detect networks that may be hidden or using dynamic channel selection.
* **SSID Detection:** Identifies the SSIDs (Service Set Identifiers) of detected networks.
* **Encryption Detection:** Detects the encryption type used by wireless networks, such as WEP, WPA, or WPA2.
* **Signal Strength Measurement:** Measures the signal strength of detected networks, allowing you to identify areas of weak coverage or interference.
* **GPS Integration:** Can be integrated with GPS devices to provide location information for detected networks.
* **Alerting:** Can be configured to send alerts when specific events occur, such as the detection of a new network or a change in signal strength.

**Use Cases:**

* **Network Surveys:** Identify wireless networks in an area and gather information about them.
* **Security Audits:** Detect unauthorized wireless networks or rogue access points.
* **Troubleshooting:** Identify network interference or coverage issues.
* **Educational Purposes:** Learn about wireless networking technologies and protocols.

**Getting Started:**

Kismet is available for various platforms, including Linux, macOS, and Windows. You can download it from the official website: [https://kismet.net/](https://kismet.net/)

Once installed, you can run Kismet and start scanning for wireless networks.

**Conclusion:**

Kismet is a valuable tool for anyone who needs to understand the wireless networks around them. Its passive scanning capabilities, wide protocol support, and GPS integration make it a powerful and versatile network detector.
